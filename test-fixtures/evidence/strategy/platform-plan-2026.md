# Vantage Freight: platform plan 2026

Consignment tracking is the capability the business runs on. It is delivered by ShipTrack, a platform we have run on our own servers since 2009.

We will move ShipTrack to a managed cloud environment across 2026 and 2027.

The flow we are protecting: a customer books a consignment, dispatch assigns it to a route, a driver scans it at each depot stop, and the customer sees a delivery confirmation.

We depend on the ShipTrack platform, the dispatch team, and the depot scanner fleet.
