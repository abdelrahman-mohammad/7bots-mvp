# Dispatch operations handbook

The dispatch team works out of the Rotterdam depot. A dispatcher owns the booking queue and a route planner owns the daily route sheet.

## Booking a consignment

1. The dispatcher opens the booking queue in ShipTrack.
2. The dispatcher checks the customer is not on credit hold.
3. ShipTrack returns a consignment number.

## Assigning a route

The route planner runs the route sheet every morning and assigns unassigned consignments to a vehicle.

We offer customers consignment booking and delivery tracking.
